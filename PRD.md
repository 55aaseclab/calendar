# Calendar / 日历

Browse a month, select a day and see the agenda. In the workbench, include dated reminders the signed-in user may read, and link back to the exact source. Standalone users can plan a single event and download a standard calendar invitation for their own calendar. Use local civil dates, English by default and Simplified Chinese on request. The app relies on the managed platform for its Home control.

# 日历

按月浏览，选择日期查看当天议程。工作台模式显示本人获准读取的有日期提醒，可打开具体来源事项。独立使用时可填写单个日程，下载标准日历邀请并导入个人日历。日期采用本地日历日期，默认英文，可切换简体中文。受管模式由平台提供返回工作台按钮。
